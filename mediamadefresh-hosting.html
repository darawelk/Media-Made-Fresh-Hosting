Is email hosting really included, or is it an add-on?

It's included on every plan, at no extra cost. You get professional email addresses at your own domain, with mailbox limits that scale up by plan.

Your site is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure that is matched 100% by renewable energy, so you get enterprise performance without an added environmental cost.
